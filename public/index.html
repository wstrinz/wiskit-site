Wiskit

A private tracker for your family

Log naps, feeds, milestones and anything else you want to remember, and share it with the people you choose. Entries sync directly between your family's own devices. There is no account, and nobody else can read what you write.

Get Wiskit

iPhone (App Store) Windows

Someone sent you a contact code? Open it on the device that has Wiskit, or install Wiskit first.

How it works

  • Your trackers live on your devices, encrypted.
  • To share, add someone by their contact code (a QR code in the app), then share a tracker with them.
  • When a device is offline, changes wait in an encrypted mailbox until it's back.
  • Link your own phone and computer so they get everything you make.
