Wiskit

A private tracker for your family

Wiskit keeps a family's trackers (naps, feeds, milestones, little sayings) on the family's own devices, shared only with the people they choose. There is no account, and nobody else can read what you write.

Wiskit is invite-only for now.

If someone sent you an invite link, open it: it has everything you need to get started.
