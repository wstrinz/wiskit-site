Wiskit

Sharing between two devices

For App Review: Wiskit's sharing needs a second device, so this recording shows it on two iPhone simulators with demo data (Sam and Alex). One minute, no sound.

  1. Sam copies their contact link (Contacts → Copy my link).
  2. Alex pastes it and adds Sam; Sam confirms Alex.
  3. Sam shares the Naps tracker with Alex; Alex accepts.
  4. Sam logs a new entry ("Down for the night"); it appears on Alex's phone.
